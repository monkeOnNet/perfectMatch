Perfect Match is a novel friend-finding/dating protocol. The protocol begins with creating a custom questionnaire and providing a message to encrypt (preferably a contact method, like an email address). The reponses are used to generate an Argon2id key, which is then hashed 220 million times and used to encrypt the message. All relevant information, including a bare-bones html decoder, is then bundled into a QR code meant to be shared in the real world, like a pheremone marker. The extreme hashing loop serves to dissuade brute-force attacks through proof-of-work.
